Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 8, 2026 · CarbonXFuture Markets, Inc., Fort Lauderdale, Florida, USA

1. Who we are

CarbonXFuture Markets, Inc. ("CarbonXFuture", "we") operates carbonxfuture.com, an institutional marketplace for carbon credits and physical waste & base oil trade. Contact: desk@carbonxfuture.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Access-request form: name, company, business email, role and volume range you submit. This is delivered to our desk by email via FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), our form-processing provider.
  • Agent CXF chat: messages you type in the assistant are transmitted to Anthropic's Claude API to generate a reply. Do not include sensitive personal information in the chat.
  • Donations: payments are processed entirely by Stripe. We never receive or store your card details. Stripe's privacy policy applies to payment data.
  • Analytics: we use privacy-friendly, aggregate analytics (Vercel Analytics) to understand site traffic. No advertising cookies.
  • Member portal: if you apply for membership, we collect account and company details, activity reports, supporting documents and listings you submit. See Section 5.
  • Transactional email: confirmations and desk notifications are sent via Resend, our email provider.

3. How we use information

We use the information above to respond to access requests and quote enquiries, operate the AI assistant, process donations, and improve the website. We do not sell personal information.

4. Sharing

Information is shared only with the service providers we use to operate the platform — FormSubmit, Anthropic, Stripe, Vercel, Supabase (database and document storage) and Resend (email) — as needed to operate the site, and where required by law. We do not sell personal information.

5. Registry and member data

Operating the CXF member portal and the CXF Registry involves processing, on the legal basis of performing our membership agreement and of our legitimate interest in market integrity: identity and company information supplied at onboarding; KYC review and sanctions-screening outcomes; monthly activity reports and their supporting evidence (weighbridge tickets, manifests, permits, production logs); listings, transfer and retirement instructions; certificate beneficiary names as directed by the retiring party; and internal audit and security logs. As described in the Registry Rules, only the non-commercial batch fields (serial, project label, contract code, vintage, quantity, status, status notes, dates, certificate serial) are public — owner identity, counterparties, prices, reports and evidence documents are not published. Members may request a generic public project label. Issuance evidence is retained for as long as the related batches or certificates remain recorded in the registry, plus any period required by law, because the permanence of the registry depends on the permanence of its evidence.

6. Retention

Access-request emails are retained for as long as needed to evaluate and manage the business relationship. Registry and issuance records follow the retention described in Section 5. You may request deletion of other personal information at any time.

7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by emailing desk@carbonxfuture.com. If you are in a jurisdiction with specific data-protection rights (e.g. GDPR, CCPA/FDBR), we will honor applicable requests.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; the effective date above reflects the latest version.

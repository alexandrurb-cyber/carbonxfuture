This registry implements CXF's internal double-issuance controls: each validated activity report supports at most one batch, every serial is unique, statuses are singular, and anyone — including your auditor — can check them here without an account. Members additionally attest that the same activity has not been claimed under another carbon program. Ownership and commercial terms are private by design; see the Registry Rules.

About this registry. CXF units are proprietary, desk-validated platform instruments of CarbonXFuture Markets, Inc. — they are not registry credits issued by Verra, Gold Standard, ACR or CAR. Batches are issued only from desk-validated activity reports with documentary evidence; serials are unique and never reused; retirement links to a numbered public certificate verifiable at /verify; revocations remain visible with a public reason — including revocation of a previously retired batch if its evidence is later found materially false. For partial transfers or retirements, a batch is split into child batches (serial suffix ‑A, ‑B, …) whose quantities always sum to the parent; the parent remains visible with status "split". Some project labels are generic at the member's request — the underlying operator identity and evidence are held privately by the desk and available to counterparties in diligence. Issuance, transfer, retirement and revocation are governed by the CXF Registry Rules.

What this confirmation is — and is not. This document confirms a recorded transfer of ownership of transferable CXF platform units (CXF Registry Rules, §3). It is not a retirement and does not constitute an emissions-offset claim by the acquirer or any other party: only a numbered CXF Retirement Certificate — created when the holder instructs the desk to permanently retire units — supports an offset claim. CXF units are proprietary, desk-validated platform instruments of CarbonXFuture Markets, Inc.; they are not registry credits issued by Verra, Gold Standard, ACR or CAR.

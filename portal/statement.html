What this statement is — and is not. This statement attests that desk-validated activity generated the transferable CXF platform units identified above (CXF Registry Rules, §2). It is not a retirement and does not constitute an emissions-offset claim by the holder or any other party: units remain transferable until retired, and only a numbered CXF Retirement Certificate — created when units are permanently retired — supports an offset claim. CXF units are proprietary, desk-validated platform instruments of CarbonXFuture Markets, Inc.; they are not registry credits issued by Verra, Gold Standard, ACR or CAR.

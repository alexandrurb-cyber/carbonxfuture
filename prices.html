Methodology. CXF marks are indicative assessments set by the CarbonXFuture desk based on documented bids, offers and counterparty indications in waste-oil carbon credits, cross-checked against relevant public market benchmarks. As desk transactions complete, they will be incorporated into the assessment basis. They are published for information only: they are not exchange-traded settlement prices, not an offer to buy or sell, and not financial advice. Registry benchmark levels (Verra, Gold Standard, ACR/CAR, Plan Vivo) are desk observations of the broader voluntary market. History accrues weekly from the date each mark is first published; revisions are not made retroactively. Read the full index methodology →

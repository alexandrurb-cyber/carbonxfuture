Desk Note: Verra Coal-Retirement Method Wins ICVCM CCP Label, Brent Slides to $103 as SK Enmove Lifts Group III Posting

November 9, 2026 · 3 min read · CarbonXFuture desk, Fort Lauderdale

Carbon markets: EU carbon permits stood at EUR 87.15/t on November 9, up 0.72% on the day (Trading Economics). In the voluntary market, Verra's VM0052 methodology for early coal plant retirement became the first energy transition methodology approved by the ICVCM as meeting the Core Carbon Principles (Verra, November 3), and an environmental group criticised the approval on November 6. Carbon Pulse headlines on November 6 also reported Verra's first credits under its carbon capture and storage methodology for a Texas project, and ICAO adding four crediting programmes to CORSIA Phase 2 while conditionally approving the Paris Agreement Crediting Mechanism. ACCUs last closed at A$38.05 (CORE Markets, November 6).

Waste oil and base oil: WTI December futures settled at $96.09/bbl on November 3, down from $99.90 on October 27, while Brent January traded at $102.80 on November 4, down from $114.91 on October 28 (Lubes'N'Greases Weekly Americas Base Oil Price Report, November 4). SK Enmove posted a 30 c/gal increase on Group II+ and III base oils effective November 1, and U.S. Group III 4 cSt prices rose about 10 c/gal; a domestic re-refiner is reportedly sold out of Group III cuts until February. Group I and II supply eased as Gulf Coast producers released inventory, with export spot indications down 5 to 10 c/gal week on week. Paulsboro's Group I plant restarted on October 26, with exports not expected until December. Gulf Coast low-sulfur diesel was $4.40/gal on November 2, from $4.89 a week earlier.

Takeaway: Crude has retreated sharply, yet Group III postings are still rising, so base oil costs are not simply following crude. Collectors and re-refiners: with Group III tight and diesel lower, benchmark pay-for-oil terms against current spot and diesel levels rather than posted prices alone. Buyers: with one re-refiner reportedly sold out until February, arrange Group III cover early.

Our indicative CXF US Gulf assessment stands at $21.50/t and CXF re-refining credits at $19.90/t — desk marks, updated November 9, 2026, unchanged from the previous update. Week-over-week change from the CXF price history was not available this run.

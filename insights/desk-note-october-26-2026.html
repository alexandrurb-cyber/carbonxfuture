Desk Note: Brent Tops $110 on Hormuz Shipping Attacks, Verra Sets Interim REDD+ Options as Group III Stays Short

October 26, 2026 · 3 min read · CarbonXFuture desk, Fort Lauderdale

Carbon markets: EU carbon permits stood at EUR 86.26/t on October 23, down 0.24% on the day (Trading Economics). In the voluntary market, Verra outlined interim options for REDD+ projects as delays slow the VM0048 rollout, and CORSIA prices were flat as the market digested over-crediting news (Carbon Pulse, October 19). The ICVCM opened a consultation on board independence and Indigenous Peoples' rights clarifications (Carbon Pulse, October 22), and Gold Standard opened a call for feedback on new activity requirements for industrial decarbonisation (Carbon Pulse, October 21). ACCUs last closed at A$37.70 (CORE Markets, October 23).

Waste oil and base oil: WTI December futures settled at $97.82/bbl on October 20, up from $94.14 on October 13, while Brent December traded at $110.75 on October 21, up from $106.07 a week earlier, with attacks on shipping in the Strait of Hormuz and Gulf of Oman cited as a driver (Lubes'N'Greases Weekly Americas Base Oil Price Report, October 21). No new posted increases were announced this week, though most Group II+ and III producers have raised postings since early September, and Group III light grades still show shortages. Group I and II export prices faced downward pressure from seasonal weakness in Latin America and Europe and lower-priced Asian cargoes. Paulsboro Refining's Group I plant is in turnaround, with spot supply unlikely before November. Gulf Coast low-sulfur diesel was $4.97/gal on October 19, from $4.83 on October 8. Consumption of Group II and re-refined base oils was generally strong, as they replaced Group III in some applications.

Takeaway: Crude-driven cost pressure is real, but Group I and II export prices are softening, so do not assume full pass-through. Collectors and re-refiners: with re-refined demand strong, review pay-for-oil terms against current spot and diesel levels rather than posted prices. Buyers: Group III remains short, so secure Q4 volumes early.

Our indicative CXF US Gulf assessment stands at $21.50/t and CXF re-refining credits at $19.90/t — desk marks, updated October 26, 2026, unchanged from October 19. Week-over-week change from the CXF price history was not available this run.
